PosePilot

A web-based platform for human motion visualisation and analysis

Abstract

Human motion and motor skills are central to domains ranging from sports and performing arts to rehabilitation, biomechanics, and Industry 5.0. Despite advances in motion capture technology, the analysis of motion data remains technically demanding: existing frameworks are either limited to qualitative visual inspection without quantitative grounding, or provide metric-based analysis within narrow, domain-specific pipelines that require proprietary software, programming expertise, or specialised hardware installations.

PosePilot addresses this gap as an open-access, browser-based motion capture viewer and analysis platform that unifies comparative visualisation with interpretable, metric-based analysis of human motion. The framework is organised into four interdependent layers – computation, visualisation, comparative analysis, and interactivity – enabling structured exploration of motion variability across subjects, conditions, and repetitions, directly in the browser without installation or coding experience. Its modular and extensible architecture supports a growing suite of domain-specific applications, making PosePilot a versatile tool for researchers, educators, and practitioners across biomechanics, sports science, performing arts, rehabilitation, and industrial ergonomics.
